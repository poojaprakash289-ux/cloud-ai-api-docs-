UX Writing

Overview

I contributed to the product experience by working closely with design and engineering teams to craft clear, concise, and intuitive microcopy across the platform. My focus was on simplifying complex workflows and ensuring users could easily understand and navigate key functionalities.

The product involved multi-step processes such as scanning, assessment, migration planning, and execution. This required careful attention to element naming, action clarity, and logical flow to guide users through each stage seamlessly.

Key Contributions

Workflow & Action Screens

Designed microcopy for multi-step workflows, ensuring users clearly understand actions such as initiating scans, reviewing assessments, and executing migrations. Emphasis was placed on reducing ambiguity and improving task completion.

Configuration & Setup Screens

Created clear and structured instructions for setup and configuration steps, helping users complete complex technical tasks with confidence and minimal errors.

Data & Insights Screens

Refined labels, tooltips, and descriptions for dashboards and reports to ensure users can easily interpret insights, recommendations, and system outputs.

Empty State Screens

Developed meaningful empty states that guide users on next steps, ensuring engagement even when no data is available.

Validation & Error Messages

Crafted precise and actionable error messages and validation prompts to help users quickly identify issues and resolve them without confusion.

User Interaction & Feedback

Improved system feedback messages (success, warning, progress states) to provide clarity and reassurance during critical operations.

Outcome

By refining microcopy and aligning it with user workflows, I helped create a more intuitive and seamless user experience. The content ensured clarity across complex processes, reduced user friction, and improved overall usability of the platform.
